## Starting to Save Chats

### How It Works
1. You chat normally
2. Click "Save Chat" button
3. The chat gets saved to browser storage
4. You can load it back later

### What Gets Saved
- All messages (yours and the AI's)
- Time stamps (when each message was sent)
- A title (like "Chat 1/15/2024")

### What I Built
- **Save button** - Saves your current chat
- **Load button** - Opens a previous chat
- **Delete button** - Removes a saved chat
- Storage system - Keeps chats on your computer
